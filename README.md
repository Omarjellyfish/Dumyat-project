🪚 Dumyat: Legacy of Wood & Water (VR)
A virtual reality journey of craftsmanship, patience, and family. Set in the heart of Damietta, Egypt, experience the tactile art of traditional carpentry, the quiet rhythm of coastal fishing, and a heartfelt father-son narrative.

📖 About the Project
Dumyat is a story-driven VR game that immerses players in the rich cultural heritage of Damietta. Famous for its master carpenters and its unique position where the Nile meets the Mediterranean Sea, the city serves as the perfect backdrop for a story about passing down traditions.

Players step into the shoes of a son learning his father's lifelong trades. Through interactive, physics-based VR mechanics, you will craft beautiful furniture, fish the local waters, and rebuild a fractured relationship one piece of wood and one cast line at a time.

✨ Key Features
Immersive VR Carpentry: Use motion controls to measure, saw, sand, carve, and assemble wood. Feel the satisfaction of building intricate Damascene-inspired furniture from raw timber.

Tactile Fishing Mechanics: Cast your line into the Mediterranean and the Nile. Master baiting, casting, and reeling in dynamic weather conditions.

Emotional Narrative: Experience a fully voiced, interactive storyline focused on generational trauma, healing, and father-son bonding.

Authentic Atmosphere: Explore a beautifully recreated, stylized environment inspired by the real streets, workshops, and coastlines of Dumyat.

Physics-Based Interactions: Every tool and object responds naturally to your hand movements, grounding you in the virtual world.
